### 7.1 Ride Pricing

The amount displayed before payment is the amount submitted to Stripe, subject to any required taxes or other disclosed charges. The code does not currently calculate demand pricing or road-route distance. The service operator must publish the applicable prices and cancellation/refund terms before launch.

### 7.2 Payment Processing

- Payment processing is subject to Stripe's terms and privacy notice.
